AI / Regulatory Intelligence Platform

Lexora

November 2026 to Present

Individual Enterprise AI Project
Lexora is an AI-powered regulatory intelligence platform designed to help organisations analyse regulatory documents, understand regulatory changes, and assess their potential impact on business processes and systems. It uses document intelligence, hybrid information retrieval, and LLM-based analysis to generate structured, evidence-backed regulatory impact assessments.

Key Capabilities:

  • Regulatory Document Ingestion: Processes regulatory documents, including PDFs and structured regulatory data.
  • Hybrid Retrieval: Combines PostgreSQL full-text search, vector similarity search using pgvector, and trigram matching to retrieve relevant regulatory information.
  • Regulatory Impact Assessment: Analyses regulatory changes and identifies potentially affected business processes, systems, and requirements.
  • Evidence-backed Analysis: Links AI-generated assessments to relevant source documents and regulatory evidence.
  • Human Review: Allows users to inspect and validate generated assessments rather than treating AI outputs as final legal decisions.
React TypeScript Python FastAPI PostgreSQL pgvector Hybrid Search Reciprocal Rank Fusion OpenRouter LLMs

Core Objective: Transform complex regulatory documents into structured, traceable, and actionable intelligence, helping organisations understand what changed, what may be affected, and why.
